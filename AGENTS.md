# Thoth community catalog

The default official catalog lives in `velrino/thoth-data`. Personal forks can publish their own workspaces under `content/workspaces/`.

Use [the catalog contribution skill](.agents/skills/thoth-catalog-contribution/SKILL.md) when adding, editing, or translating diagram content. Use [the catalog review skill](.agents/skills/thoth-catalog-review/SKILL.md) to review the story, layout, and readability of published diagrams. These instructions apply to any assistant or editor. Follow [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) for review, source attribution, and contributor rights.
